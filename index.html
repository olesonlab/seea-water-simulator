SEEA CF 2028 — Water Valuation Simulator

This simulator tests three recommendations in the proposed SEEA Central Framework update for water monetary asset accounts. Adjust extraction and recharge to see the annual flow value Ft and the depletion charge Dt diverge (Rec 2 — Valuation Targets), watch OCV reconcile the two (Rec 3 — MSUT and Asset Account Reconciliation), and adjust the substitute ceiling to see when it caps Dt as the stock nears exhaustion (Rec 8 — Valuation at the Extremes).

Key relationships: Ft = Rt × qt (MSUT flow value)  |  Dt = p* × max(0, qt − gt) (depletion charge, eq. D.5 — Rec 2 names Dt a valuation target)  |  Rec 8 (Valuation at the Extremes): Dt capped at the substitute technology cost as the stock nears exhaustion  |  OCV = Ft − qt × p* (Rec 3, MSUT / asset account reconciliation)

Notation: this tool uses the shadow price p* throughout, for simplicity. The guidance note’s eq. D.5 uses p̄, the operational proxy for p*, since p* is rarely estimated directly in practice (§3.5.1). Results here approximate what compilers would produce using p̄.
Annex E worked examples (same formulas, real numbers)
Ex 1 — Renewable aquifer, qt=10 > gt=8 km³/yr: Dt=Int$500M. The constant-rent perpetuity decouples p̄ from Rt, so OCVt=+Int$2,500M even under over-extraction.
Ex 2 — Fossil aquifer, gt=0: all extraction depletes. Dt=Int$5,000M exceeds Ft=Int$4,000M — wealth falls faster than income is earned.
Ex 3 — Over-extracted surface water: a smaller over-extraction volume than Ex 2, but a higher p̄=Int$2.25/m³, gives both a larger Dt (Int$6,750M) and a larger OCVt gap.
Ex 4 — Near exhaustion (S=16 km³, ΔS/S=31%): uncapped Dt would be Int$31,250M. Rec 8's substitute ceiling (psubstitute=Int$2.00/m³) caps it at Int$10,000M — the scenario this tool's substitute-ceiling slider is built to reproduce.
Ex 5 — OCVt decomposition: falls from +Int$2,500M to +Int$1,000M as Rt drops from Int$0.50 to Int$0.20/m³, tracking the gap between current rent and the capitalized shadow price.
Ex 6 — qt=8 < gt=10 km³/yr: the raw Dt calculation is negative, floored to zero (Case 3). The addition instead raises NDP by Int$320M.
